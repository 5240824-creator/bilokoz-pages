Who I am

Certified Success MindsetMaps™ Coach (Robert Dilts) and consultant; M.A. in Psychology, clinical specialisation. I work at the synergy of temperament neurobiology, coaching and methods of working with state.

I specialise in methods that engage the deep structures of experience, not only awareness. My leading protocol at present is MoM (Andrew Austin): not an interpretation of the metaphor, but an exploration of the space the metaphor lives in.

Education

Three master's degrees — law, management and psychology (clinical). The master’s dissertation in psychology is “Psychotherapy of Allergic Reactions by Psychological Methods”. Research and practice of work with the psychogenic component of allergic reactions, without cancelling medical protocols.

Legal and management training give a habit of precision in wording, and of telling a structure apart from the story told about that structure.

Methodological position

My approach comes together from two lines.

Western — technological and checkable: MoM and Integral Eye Movement Therapy (Andrew Austin), New Code NLP, which I studied twice with John Grinder himself, Ericksonian hypnosis, Core Transformation, Frank Farrelly's provocative therapy, Robert Dilts and his coaching.

Eastern — work with state and body: Ho'oponopono, the tradition of Morrnah Simeona and Hew Len, and the qigong of Xu Mingtang, where I hold a certification.

A note on NLP.
NLP in its original sense is applied epistemology and the modelling of patterns of genius: a description of what Fritz Perls, Virginia Satir and Milton Erickson did intuitively, with the aim of making it reproducible. New Code shifts the focus from changing behaviour to managing a high performance state. Neuro-linguistic psychotherapy (NLPt) is today a recognised modality within the scientific community.

Certification

Most recent: Success MindsetMaps™ Coach — certificate issued by MindsetMaps International Inc. Certified MindsetMaps™ Coach.

The method is Success Factor Modeling (SFM), authored by Robert Dilts, co-founder of MindsetMaps International. He is among the signatories of the certificate, together with Mickey A. Feher (co-founder) and Elena Otekina (partner). My other certificates and diplomas will stay where they are — on the bookshelf.

Who this site is for

For those who value self-understanding, precision, structure and depth — rather than miraculous promises. Here you can understand the approach, study the materials and take a temperament profile.

Personal work is limited. I have a varied professional life, and private practice deliberately remains one segment of it. Getting in is realistic, but sometimes you have to wait. Often a short conversation of 20–30 minutes is enough to see whether the work is needed at all.
