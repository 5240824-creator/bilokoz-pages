How it works

What Health Scan is

Health Scan is an assessment instrument within MindsetMaps, developed by Robert Dilts as part of Success Factor Modeling. It measures not character but your current way of thinking about a goal, a strategy and a context — where the resource is, where the tension is, where the blind spot is.

Unlike FET, which describes how the nervous system is built (what does not change), Health Scan describes how you currently assemble decisions and where the thinking can be extended. Two different instruments: they do not replace each other, they complement each other.

What the MindsetMaps International certification confirms:

  • applying the MindsetMaps™ Inventory
  • supporting an optimal mindset for a meta goal
  • implementing the SFM Circle of Success™
  • using tools to adjust meta, macro and micro mindset

A limit. With a psychiatric diagnosis, psychiatric supervision or prescribed psychiatric medication, the work is possible only with the written consent of your treating physician. I may ask you to coordinate the format with your specialist, or decline if I consider the format unsuitable.

Frequent questions
How does coaching differ from therapy?

Therapy works with what gets in the way — trauma, fears, patterns of tension. Coaching works with how to improve — strategy, thinking, goals, context. In practice they interweave. If it becomes clear that therapy is what's needed, I will say so directly and recommend turning to a specialist who provides it.

Can I take only the Health Scan, without sessions?

Yes. Level 1 is self-administered, $25 on the platform. If you want help with interpretation and a more personalised report — level 2 (€150). The full package with follow-up work — level 3 (€1500).

How many sessions are usually needed after the standard package?

It varies. Often the three sessions in the package are enough — especially if you came with a specific question and the Health Scan gave a clear picture. Sometimes a continuation is needed; that is your decision, not mine.
