How to write

Briefly and to the point. It helps me if the first message has three things:

  • What is happening. What is it like to be in your position? One or two sentences, without a page of backstory.
  • What you have already tried. This shortens the path.
  • What you want from the meeting. Even if the wording is imprecise.

If you don't know what to write — write as it is. We'll sort it out.

Before you write

Who I do not work with. With a psychiatric diagnosis, psychiatric supervision or prescribed psychiatric medication, the work is possible only with the written consent of your treating physician. I may ask you to coordinate the format with your specialist, or decline if I consider the format unsuitable. Coaching and consulting do not replace treatment and can interfere with it if they bypass the person looking after you.

If the state is acute — thoughts of self-harm, panic, crisis — what is needed is emergency help, not a consultation. Contact the local emergency or crisis service where you actually are. In Israel, for example: ambulance 101, the emotional first aid line ERAN 1201 — around the clock, with Russian-speaking volunteers.

Format and availability

I work online. Personal work is limited. Private practice deliberately remains one segment and a particular area of what I do. Getting in is realistic — sometimes you have to wait. Often a short conversation of 20–30 minutes is enough to see whether the work is needed at all.

If you would like to start with something open that does not require my time — there is the FET temperament profile and Health Scan.
