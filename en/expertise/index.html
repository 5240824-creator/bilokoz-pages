Umbrella of methods

Work with deep patterns, getting stuck in crises, emotional blocks and psychosomatic components — taking work with allergies as an example, among others. I use precise tools matched to a particular architecture of thinking — not one method for every case, but the one that fits your task.

New Code NLP
John Grinder

Work with the unconscious as a partner rather than an object. The focus shifts from mechanically changing behaviour to managing a high performance state: not installing the right reaction, but assembling the conditions in which it arises by itself.

Article in preparation
Classical NLP
Grinder, Bandler

Applied epistemology: the modelling of patterns of genius. It grew from describing what Fritz Perls, Virginia Satir and Milton Erickson did intuitively, with the aim of making it reproducible. Neuro-linguistic psychotherapy (NLPt) is today a recognised modality within the scientific community.

Ericksonian hypnosis
Jean Becchio, M.R. Ginzburg

Indirect suggestion, trance as an instrument of change. M.R. Ginzburg is a Russian doctor of psychological sciences of the Ericksonian school; Jean Becchio is a French physician and hypnotherapist, author of the theme of activation de conscience.

Article in preparation
Provocative therapy
Frank Farrelly

An approach in which the practitioner takes the position of advocate for the problem and, through provocative humour, returns the client to their own strength. Useful, interesting, effective — especially where rational approaches hit a ceiling.

Article in preparation
Core Transformation
Connirae, Steve and Mark Andreas

Work with the deep structures of identity through access to core states. Founders — Connirae and Steve Andreas; the younger generation — Mark Andreas, who also runs current webinars.

Article in preparation
The Eastern line
Hew Len (Morrnah Simeona), Xu Mingtang

Ho'oponopono — the practice of total responsibility; I consider Hew Len my teacher, and the tradition originates with Morrnah Simeona. It is a genuinely interesting subject: a strikingly structured, consistent and precise system has been turned by much of the Russian-speaking internet into cleansing mantras, which the method emphatically is not. Xu Mingtang's qigong — work with state and body through the Chinese tradition.

02
Instrumental assessment

Temperament profile (FET) — testing

A neurobiological model by Irina Trofimova (McMaster). Not a personality typology, but a map of how your nervous system processes situations, energy, risk and load.

What you get as a result:

  • Portrait of temperament — how your nervous system is built
  • Points of overload — and particularities that need compensating
  • Zones of genius — where your nervous system works at its strongest

For developing projects and self-realisation, profiling is recommended. It complements the coaching assessment through MindsetMaps well.

Three levels

  • L1 — free online questionnaire on this site + basic report
  • L2 — written report (€100)
  • L3 — personal consultation on the results (€200), usually 1–2 hours

04
A separate area

Working with allergies

Allergies are the subject of my master’s dissertation “Psychotherapy of Allergic Reactions by Psychological Methods”. A subject at the meeting point of psychology, neurobiology and immunology. I work with the psychogenic component of the reaction and with how a person lives alongside an allergy: anticipatory anxiety, avoidance, the tension around the subject.

Important. An allergy is a medical diagnosis. This work does not act on the immune mechanism and does not cancel a diet, allergen elimination, prescribed medication or the need to carry adrenaline in an auto-injector. Nothing I say is grounds for stopping any of these.

  • Individual work
  • Short questions and consultations through Telegram
  • L1 — first consultation: going through your question — €100, usually 1–2 hours
