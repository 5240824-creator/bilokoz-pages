Instrumental assessment

Temperament profile FET

FET — Functional Ensemble of Temperament — a neurochemical and psychological model by Irina Trofimova (McMaster) linking temperament traits to psychiatric symptoms. Not a personality typology, but a map of how your nervous system processes situations, energy, risk and load.

Result

What you get at the end

Not a psychological type and not a character. Three concrete layers that any further work rests on — your own, or with a specialist.

01

Portrait of temperament

How your nervous system is built: speed of reaction, tolerance of load, sensitivity, dominant modalities. Not a good-or-bad assessment — a fact about how you are made.

02

Risks and particularities

The points where your nervous system tends towards overload or breakdown. Knowing them means building environment and load so as to compensate for them in advance, rather than after a crisis.

03

Zones of genius

Where your nervous system works better than most. Usually a person is not aware of these zones — they feel natural. Careers, projects and self-realisation can be built on them.

A temperament profile is not a diagnosis. FET describes how the nervous system is built, not the presence or absence of a disorder. The questionnaire is a research instrument: it does not detect illness, does not replace medical examination and is not a basis for medical decisions.

For developing projects, self-realisation and growing income profiling is recommended but not required. It sits well alongside the coaching assessment through MindsetMaps (Robert Dilts): temperament is how the system is built; MindsetMaps is how you currently think. Two different instruments that complement each other.

Frequent questions

FAQ

How does FET differ from MBTI and other tests?

FET is grounded in neurochemistry rather than in self-description of styles. It measures parameters of behavioural regulation tied to specific neurotransmitter systems — dopamine, serotonin, noradrenaline and others. Not a type, but a profile.

What do I get for free?

An automatic report with a basic description of the profile after taking the questionnaire on this site. Access to Irina Trofimova's materials for going deeper on your own.

Why a written report if there is an automatic one?

The automatic report is a technical read-out by scale. The written one is an interpretation of your specific profile, taking into account the interactions between scales, the risks and the recommendations.

Can I do all of it on my own, without a consultation?

Yes. Level 1 plus Trofimova's materials give a good basis for understanding on your own. Reading these results properly, however, takes a fairly long training: a number or a scale on its own sometimes means something good, sometimes something less good, sometimes nothing at all — they all work in an ensemble with the others. One high score in one ensemble is a different thing from another high score in a different ensemble. A consultation is for going through the results personally and applying them to a specific question.
