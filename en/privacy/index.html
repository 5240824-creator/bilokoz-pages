Legal information

Privacy

In short: this site does not collect your data, does not run analytics and does not show advertising. Below is what happens technically.

What the site does not do

  • Does not collect personal data: there are no registrations or subscriptions. The one exception is the FET temperament questionnaire, covered in its own section below.
  • Does not use analytics or visitor counters.
  • Does not carry advertising and does not pass data to ad networks.
  • Does not set tracking cookies.

What is stored in your browser

On all pages — one entry: the colour theme you chose (dark or light). It is kept in your browser's localStorage, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed together with the site data in your browser settings. What the FET questionnaire page stores is described in its own section below.

External services

The site makes no external requests when a page loads. Fonts, images and stylesheets are served from this same domain. No third-party service — Google Fonts included — receives your IP address or browser data. The exception is the FET questionnaire: its answers go to my calculation server, see below.

External navigation happens only when you click:

  • Telegram — only on a link click. Once you are in Telegram, Telegram's privacy policy applies, not this site's.
  • YouTube — only on a link click. No video is embedded in the pages, so without your click no request reaches YouTube.

Hosting

The site is static: it consists of prepared files with no database and no server logic; only the FET questionnaire has a server of its own. The hosting provider may keep technical access logs (IP address, request time, browser type) for security and availability. I neither request nor use those logs as personal data.

FET temperament questionnaire

The FET questionnaire page is the only place on this site where you enter anything. Your answers to the 77 statements are sent to the server, the result is computed there, and the page shows you the report. Stored together with the answers: a name label, age and sex if you gave them; your email only if you ticked the separate consent to be contacted; the time of completion and answer timing; IP address, country and network owner; browser and device type, language and time zone. Country and network owner are looked up from the IP address on my server, in a local database, without calling third-party services (IP Geolocation by DB-IP). If the address belongs to a hosting or VPN service, or the browser time zone does not match the country of the address, the record gets a service note "probably VPN"; this does not affect the test result.

There is no registration. So that you can pause and come back, your browser (localStorage) keeps your answers, the form, the attempt ticket and the finished report. The ticket is valid for 30 days. You can erase all of it from the browser with the "Delete test data from this browser" button under the report, or in your browser settings.

Why: to show you the report and to let me go through the result with you in a consultation if you ask for one. Dmitry receives a service notification about each completed questionnaire with the profile chart, without the name or the email; it is delivered through Telegram. Apart from that, results are not passed to anyone and are not published. Records are kept for one year from completion and then anonymised; in the encrypted backups they remain for up to 30 more days. To have a record deleted earlier, message me on Telegram with the name label or the approximate time of completion.

Correspondence and work

Contact goes through Telegram. What you write to me in direct messages stays between us and is stored in your Telegram account and mine.

Everything that happens in consultations and in profiling is confidential. Profiling materials and session records are not published. If an anonymised example is needed for an article or a teaching breakdown, I will ask for separate written consent; a refusal changes nothing in the work itself.

Site content

The texts are my own. Using the contents of this site to train language models is not permitted — the corresponding restrictions are set out in robots.txt and llms.txt. Quoting with a link to the source is fine.
