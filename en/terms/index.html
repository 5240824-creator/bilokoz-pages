Terms of work

Please read before the first payment. By booking, you agree to these terms.

1 The status I work in

I work as a coach, consultant and methodologist. I hold higher education in psychology, including a master's degree with a clinical specialisation obtained outside Israel. This is information about education, not a claim to a professional licence.

I am not listed in the Register of Psychologists of Israel and I do not provide services in the status of a licensed psychologist, psychotherapist or physician.

2 Boundaries of the work and safety

This is not medical or psychiatric care and not an emergency service. I do not make diagnoses, do not prescribe or withdraw medication, and do not conduct psychotherapy of disorders.

With a psychiatric diagnosis, psychiatric supervision or prescribed psychiatric medication, the work is possible only with the written consent of your treating physician. I may ask you to coordinate the format with your specialist, or decline if I consider the format unsuitable.

Nothing I say is grounds to change or discontinue prescribed treatment, a diet, allergen elimination or carrying adrenaline in an auto-injector. Those decisions are made by your physician, not by me.

In an acute crisis, or where there is immediate danger to yourself or others, contact the local emergency or crisis service where you actually are.

3 Price, cancellation and rescheduling

I name the price before payment. Payment buys a specific agreed meeting or package, not ongoing support.

The agreed time is reserved for you. Rescheduling or cancellation — no later than 24 hours in advance; later than that, and in case of a no-show, the meeting is payable in full. If you arrive late, the meeting is not extended and ends at the agreed time. Rescheduling or a partial refund is at my discretion.

The 24-hour rule applies subject to mandatory consumer rights that cannot be limited by agreement. Where applicable law provides for a withdrawal period for a distance contract, work begins before that period ends at your express request.

I work in good faith and within my competence, but I do not guarantee a particular outcome, the disappearance of a symptom, or a number of meetings.

4 Either of us can stop

You — at any moment: not answering a question, declining an exercise, ending the meeting, ending the work. No explanation required.

I — before or during a meeting, if the format does not suit the request, the request falls outside my competence, another specialist is needed, or continuing is not appropriate. I am not obliged to disclose detailed reasons, but I can recommend where to turn. In that case payment for the meeting not held and for the unprovided part of a prepaid package is refunded in full. Such a refusal is not a judgement about you.

If the work is already under way, I give notice of its ending in advance and, where the situation calls for it, suggest a further route.

5 Confidentiality

The content of our meetings is confidential. I do not pass it to third parties without your consent — except where disclosure is required by applicable law or is necessary in connection with an immediate danger to a person's life or safety.

Anonymised examples for articles and teaching — only with separate consent; a refusal changes nothing in the work. Audio or video recording of a meeting by either party — only by prior agreement.

Telegram, Zoom, Google Meet and WhatsApp are means of communication. I cannot guarantee the absolute security of a third-party service or of your device; please arrange a private place for the conversation. How I handle your data is set out below.

6 International format and applicable law

The work is conducted remotely. I permanently reside and run a private practice in Israel, but during a meeting the parties may be located in different states. Before the first meeting, please tell me your country of habitual residence and, if it differs, the country you are physically in during the consultation.

If the requirements of a particular jurisdiction do not allow the work to be conducted in the agreed format, or call for regulatory or technical infrastructure I do not have, I may decline before the work begins, with a full refund.

To the extent the parties are free to choose the applicable law, the relationship is governed by the substantive law of the State of Israel. That choice does not limit mandatory consumer rights that cannot be excluded by agreement.

7 Your data

For the work I need your contact details and what you tell me yourself in correspondence and in meetings. Payments are recorded in accounting, as the law requires.

Correspondence stays in whichever messenger we use. My working notes are kept by me and are not published anywhere. Health information, if you share it, I record only to the extent needed for the work.

Accounting documents are kept for the periods established by law. Working notes are kept only for as long as is reasonably necessary for current or possible further work and for meeting applicable legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

You may request information about the data I hold, a copy of it, its correction or its deletion — in the cases and to the extent provided by applicable law. Write on Telegram or by email.

8 Referrals and reimbursement in Israel

A referral (הפניה) to me from an Israeli health fund is not possible: I am not on the funds' contracted lists. My services are not reimbursable — neither under the health basket, nor under health funds' supplementary insurance programmes (שב״ן), nor under private insurers' policies.

Reimbursement of such costs is provided for appointments with licensed specialists — psychologists, psychiatrists and social workers listed in the corresponding registers. The receipt I issue confirms payment for a service and nothing more: it is not suitable for a claim to a health fund, for a tax deduction, or for an insurance claim.

For the same reason I do not issue reports or certificates for bodies that require an assessment by a licensed psychologist: educational placement committees (ועדת השמה), courts, the army, the driver licensing authority or fitness-for-work committees.

By booking and paying for a meeting after reading these terms, you agree to them.
